Movement
- run
- jump 
- dash (small flat cooldown, limited to one in the air)
- descend through platforms
- Grapple to platforms or walls
	- If you grapple and then cancel, preserve momentum

Fighting
- Melee attack (short range hitbox with some knockback)
	- charge to increase range and damage, reduced movement speed while charging
- ranged attack (long range projectile)
	- Moves horizontally at constant speed
	- charge to increase damage and give explosion radius, massively reduced movement speed while charging
- Shield (reduce damage of next hit taken. If you successfully block an attack, your next attack deals bonus damage)

Possible movement/fighting combos if we have time
- Grapple damage (if you grapple a solid wall or the ground, landing on the surface creates an explosion)
- Grapple people (if you grapple your enemy, it pulls them to you, stunning them)
- Dash attack (dashing while holding a charge attack increases range)
- Parry (if you dash through an enemy, your next attack deals bonus damage)


AI strategies
	- learning both improves strategies and improves the ability to swap between them
- Aggressive 
	- Movement: Grapple/dash close to enemy
		- Willing to tank a couple uncharged shots
	- Attacking: If close to enemy, repeatedly melee attack
		- continues to engage, even if it loses a bit of health
	- Swapping Method: Grapple away from combat 
- Sneaky
	- Movement: Carefully approach enemy, sometimes while holding a charged melee attack
		- Use dashes & short-ranged grapples to dodge ranged attacks
	- Attacking: grapple close to enemy, melee attack, dash out, ranged attack
		- Shield if enemy approaches
	- Swapping Method: Only after landing a shot. Suddenly swap to Aggressive, Passive, or Camper
		
- Passive 
	- Movement: Stay away from opponent
	- Attacking: Chip away at opponent's health with ranged attack
		- tries to snipe you if you are in the air
	- Swapping Method: Only after shooting a projectile. Immediately runs after the projectile and swaps to Aggressive or Sneaky mode
- Camper
	- Movement: if enemy gets close, run away while shooting ranged shots
	- Attacking: charge ranged attack and wait for enemy
		- Try to time attack to hit the opponent if opponent is in the air
	- Swapping Method: When the enemy gets close, swaps to Aggressive or Sneaky mode instead of running away
